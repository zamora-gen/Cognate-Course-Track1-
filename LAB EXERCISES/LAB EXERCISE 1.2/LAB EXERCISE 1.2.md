This Arduino LED circuit demonstrates a synchronized lighting pattern using 11 LEDs connected to an Arduino Uno. The LEDs are programmed to light up from the outermost sides moving toward the center, creating a mirror effect. The sequence begins with LED 1 and LED 11 turning on simultaneously, followed by LED 2 and LED 10, LED 3 and LED 9, LED 4 and LED 8, and LED 5 and LED 7. After these paired LEDs meet near the center, LED 6 lights up alone as the midpoint of the sequence. The pattern then reverses, moving outward again from the center by turning on LED 5 and LED 7, LED 4 and LED 8, LED 3 and LED 9, LED 2 and LED 10, and finally LED 1 and LED 11. This looping effect creates a smooth and symmetrical running light animation that demonstrates the use of arrays, for loops, and digital output control in Arduino programming.
